<article class="case-study" id="data-systems">
  <div class="case-meta">Professional practice · Data & operations</div>
  <h2>Operational data systems</h2>
  <p><strong>Challenge:</strong> Important reporting depended on inconsistent records, manual preparation, and operational knowledge held by too few people.</p>
  <p><strong>Response:</strong> I administered the underlying data systems, strengthened validation and governance, automated recurring preparation, and translated real program workflows into reporting structures teams could use without an analyst standing beside them.</p>
  <p><strong>Impact:</strong> The work became more repeatable, easier to audit, and less vulnerable to staff transitions or undocumented decisions.</p>
  <div class="evidence-line"><span>Evidence being prepared</span> Process maps · sample dictionaries · automation patterns · dashboard concepts</div>
</article>
