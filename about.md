My professional background spans data and impact management, reporting operations, systems administration, automation, documentation, and security-minded analysis. I am most useful in the space between technical systems and the people accountable for making them work.

That often means entering an environment where the process is partly documented, the data reflects years of operational compromise, and the important questions cannot wait for perfect conditions. I learn the system, locate the failure points, and build a path from raw activity to information people can defend and act upon.

At Wilmington Alliance, I administered and supported Bonterra Apricot as a core nonprofit data and case-management platform. My work included user access, permissions, workflow design, reporting, data quality, documentation, and troubleshooting across workforce development and community engagement programs. I also translated a multi-partner employment process into trackable milestones covering readiness, applications, interviews, employment outcomes, and retention follow-up.

Outside formal roles, I maintain the **Devious Designs Applied Technology Lab**, an independent record of embedded hardware, radio, wireless systems, defensive research, and field computing. It is less a collection of gadgets than a record of questions: what worked, what failed, what changed, and what the next experiment should test.

My approach is analytical without becoming detached from the human consequences of the work. I value privacy, freedom, ethics, equality, useful documentation, and technology that expands people’s ability to act rather than obscuring it.

## Current direction

- Data and impact systems for mission-driven organizations
- Apricot administration, configuration, reporting, and support
- Workflow automation and durable reporting practices
- Applied hardware and field-system research
- Responsible adoption of AI tools
- Teaching technical ideas without unnecessary gatekeeping
